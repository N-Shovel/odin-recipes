Piyanggang Chicken

Description

Piyanggang Chicken is a traditional Tausug dish made with chicken simmered in a rich, aromatic sauce. Its signature is the unique black charred coconut sauce, which gives the dish a smoky flavor and striking color.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, cut into serving pieces
  • 2 cups grated coconut
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, minced
  • 2 stalks lemongrass, bruised
  • 2 tablespoons turmeric powder
  • 2 to 3 chili peppers, sliced
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Toast the grated coconut in a dry pan over medium heat until deeply browned and nearly black, stirring often.
  2. Grind or blend the charred coconut with the onion, garlic, ginger, turmeric, and chili peppers to make a thick paste.
  3. Season the chicken with salt and pepper, then sear it in a pot until lightly browned.
  4. Add the coconut paste and lemongrass, stirring to coat the chicken.
  5. Pour in the coconut milk, cover the pot, and simmer until the chicken is tender and the sauce is thick.
  6. Adjust the seasoning and serve the Piyanggang Chicken hot with steamed rice.
