Kinalas

Description

Kinalas is a hearty Bicolano noodle soup known for its rich, savory broth made from tender beef or pork parts. It is commonly served with noodles, aromatics, and a flavorful meat sauce.

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef shank or pork parts
  • 400 g fresh or dried egg noodles
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 4 cups water or beef broth
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Spring onions and chili oil, for serving

Steps

  1. Place the meat, onion, garlic, ginger, and water or broth in a pot.
  2. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer until the meat is tender.
  3. Remove the meat, let it cool slightly, and shred it into bite-sized pieces.
  4. Season the broth with fish sauce, soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
  5. Cook the noodles according to the package directions, then drain them.
  6. Place the noodles and shredded meat in bowls, pour over the hot broth, and garnish with spring onions and chili oil.
